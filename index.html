HOW IT WORKS

From a messy Inbox to a vault you can find things in.

Independent musicians run a small business out of downloads, email attachments and desktop folders. Vault Clerk works like the clerk at a bank vault: it takes what you hand over, puts it in the right box, and brings back what you need when a request comes in.

  1. I

    Inbox

    Point it at a messy Inbox folder. It suggests where each file belongs in your vault, with a short reason.

  2. II

    File

    You check the plan and say yes. It copies files into your vault, and your Inbox keeps every original.

  3. III

    Request

    Paste a sync brief, live offer or collab request. It builds the package: filled paperwork, the right audio and a rights summary.

  4. IV

    Pitch

    Turn chosen projects into a password-protected listening page that you upload to your own web host.

WHAT IT DOES

One vault for the business side of your music.

Vault Clerk works with the vault folder you already keep. It learns how you file from the corrections you make.

INGEST & SEARCH

Filing that learns how you file

  • Smart filing suggestions, each with a reason
  • Spots exact duplicates and old versions
  • Suggests clean names for bounces and stems
  • Searches inside PDF, Word, Excel and text files
  • Reads scans and photos of documents on Mac
REQUEST

Answer a brief in one go

  • Matches a brief to your catalogue and checks rights status
  • Fills your own templates: contracts, quotes, invoices, split sheets and riders
  • Or pick one of four ready-made template looks
  • Uses your details only after you've checked them
  • Builds the package for you to send yourself
PITCH & RIGHTS

Pitch what's clear to pitch

  • Marks each project LIVE or DARK, with the reason
  • Password-protected pitch rooms that expire on the date you set
  • You choose what people can do: listen, download, originals or stems
  • You choose what they see: catalogue, ISRC, writers, lyrics
  • DARK projects share preview copies only, never masters

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

What is Vault Clerk?

Vault Clerk is a desktop app for independent musicians. It suggests where each file in a messy Inbox belongs in your vault, searches inside your documents, and answers briefs such as sync requests and live offers with filled paperwork, the right audio and a rights summary. You approve every step.

Do my files go online?

No. Vault Clerk runs on your own computer and opens in your browser at a private local address. Nothing is uploaded, and the app needs no account and no cloud.

Will Vault Clerk delete or move my files?

No. It copies files into your vault and your Inbox keeps every original. It never deletes anything: duplicates and junk go to a Trash folder inside your vault that only you empty. Every action is shown as a plan first, and nothing happens until you say yes.

Does Vault Clerk use AI?

Filing works mostly from clear rules and how you've filed before, and it learns from your corrections. A local AI model is optional and runs on your own computer. Nothing is sent to a cloud AI, and your identity and tax drawer is never sent to any AI.

Can Vault Clerk send emails or share files for me?

No. It builds the package or pitch room, and you send or upload it yourself. Pitch rooms are password-protected pages that expire on the date you set, uploaded to your own web host.

Which computers does Vault Clerk run on?

macOS 10.15 or later on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, and Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).

How do I get Vault Clerk?

Vault Clerk is in an early beta. Apply for the beta and we invite people in small groups; the download link is emailed to invited testers only. Beta testers get a free licence at launch. The app will be licensed to you, for good, and prices haven't been set yet.

Who makes Vault Clerk?

Vault Clerk is made by Craving Sofa Entertainment, the independent music house that also makes GenreMatch.
